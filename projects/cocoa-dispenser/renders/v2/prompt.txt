The attached CAD renders show version 2 of a 3D-printed push-button cocoa dispenser for latte art with a rotating shutter. It has eight printed parts, no bought parts:
- Stand: a flat ring (105 mm) that rests on a cup rim, with a slot open to the front for the stencil and a low rim on top.
- Stencil: a thin disc (82 mm) with a heart cut-out in the centre, a 1.5 mm lip around its edge, and a pull tab with a round finger hole.
- Body: a cylinder hopper (72 mm wide, 75 mm tall) on a wide flat flange (98 mm). Its floor is a sieve plate with small round holes in six wedge-shaped sectors (like six pie slices), solid between them. In the centre a short round post.
- Rotor: a thin disc (47 mm) with six open wedge sectors and six solid ones, a tall round boss in the centre with a square hole on top, and two low radial vanes. It lies on the sieve plate.
- Rod: a long square rod (6 mm), its lower part visibly twisted like a push drill, the upper part straight.
- Lid: a flat disc that pushes into the top of the body, with a square centre hole and a shallow round recess for the spring.
- Spring: a small printed wave spring (22 mm wide, 38 mm tall): stacked wavy rings touching at their crests.
- Button: a small cylinder cap (32 mm) with a shallow finger dimple on top.
